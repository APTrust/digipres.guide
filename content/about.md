## About the toolkit

This website is the companion to *Digital Preservation Toolkit for Organizations with Limited Resources*, published by the Council on Library and Information Resources (CLIR) in its Pocket Burgundy series. The toolkit is for archives, libraries, museums, historical societies, and anyone else caring for digital collections without a preservation department or a large budget.

The website has the same content as the publication, arranged to use while you work. The [pathfinder](/start/) asks what you're working with and what you can put toward it, then points you to the steps, tools, and sections that matter most. The full toolkit is always one click away, in four phases: LEARN, READY, CURATE, and PRESERVE.

## Privacy

We collect as little as we can.

- **Checklists:** what you tick off is saved in your own browser, on your own device. It's never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data erases it.
- **Feedback:** the feedback form sends what you type, and the page you were on, to a Google Form held by APTrust. Only the toolkit team can read it. Your email address is optional, and we use it only to reply.
- **Visit counts:** we count page views with [Matomo](https://matomo.org/), an open-source analytics tool, on a server the project controls rather than a commercial service. It doesn't use cookies, it shortens IP addresses so they can't identify you, and it counts nothing if your browser asks sites not to track you. It records the address of each page you view, and which features you use (for example, opening a glossary term or ticking a checklist item), but never what you type into search or any form. Because each path through the pathfinder has its own address, those counts tell us which paths people use, without knowing who chose them.

The site doesn't use cookies, and apart from the Matomo counter it doesn't load anything from other servers.
